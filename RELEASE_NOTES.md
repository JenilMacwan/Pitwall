# PitWall v1.2.0 Release Notes 🏁

We are excited to announce **PitWall v1.2.0**, featuring significant UI refinements and the highly anticipated dynamic theming engine.

## 🚀 What's New?

### 🎨 Dynamic Theming & Dark Mode
Personalize your experience with our new robust theming engine:
- **Real-time Switching**: Toggle between Light and Dark modes instantly without app restarts.
- **System Sync**: Automatically follow your device's system-wide theme settings.
- **Theme Picker**: A new intuitive selector in Settings to manage your visual preferences.

---

### ✨ UI/UX Overhaul
We've polished the interface to give you the most premium F1 experience:
- **Standings Screen**: Redesigned layouts for Drivers and Constructors for better legibility at a glance.
- **Results Screen**: Improved visual hierarchy and data presentation for past races.
- **Component Polish**: Updated `AvatarCircle`, `NewsCard`, and `F1BottomNavigationBar` with smoother animations and modern styling.

---

## 🛠️ Bug Fixes & Technical Improvements
- **Schedule Logic**: Fixed a critical bug in the Race Schedule that caused inconsistencies in session displays.
- **Navigation**: Optimized the Bottom Navigation Bar for better responsiveness and glassmorphism effects.
- **Refined Data Rendering**: Improved how news and results are presented within their respective components.

---

## 📲 How to Install
1. Download the `PitWall-v1.2.0.apk`.
2. Open the file on your Android device.
3. If prompted, allow "Installation from Unknown Sources".
4. Open **PitWall** and enjoy the season!

# PitWall v1.1.0 Release Notes 🏁


We are excited to announce the release of **PitWall v1.1.0**, packed with highly requested features and a fresh look to enhance your Formula 1 experience.

## 🚀 What's New?

### 📅 Advanced Calendar Sync
Never miss a green light again! You can now synchronize the entire 2026 F1 schedule directly to your device's calendar.
- **Sync All Races**: Add the complete season from Round 1 to the finale.
- **Sync Upcoming**: Intelligently adds only future races to keep your calendar tidy.
- **Clear Events**: A one-tap option in Settings to safely remove all PitWall-synced events.

---

### ⚙️ Enhanced Settings Hub
A completely redesigned Settings screen for easier navigation:
- **Appearance**: Quick access to Theme and Dark Mode toggles.
- **Sync Management**: New centralized controls for your calendar integrations.
- **About Section**: Detailed developer info and app versioning.

---

## 🛠️ Technical Improvements
- **Precision Timing**: Implemented `java.time` APIs for robust conversion of UTC race times to your local device time.
- **Architecture**: Leveraged Hilt for better state management in the Settings and Schedule screens.
- **Parsing**: Enhanced HTML scraping for more reliable news delivery.

---

## 📲 How to Install
1. Download the `PitWall-v1.1.0.apk`.
2. Open the file on your Android device.
3. If prompted, allow "Installation from Unknown Sources".
4. Open **PitWall** and enjoy the season!
