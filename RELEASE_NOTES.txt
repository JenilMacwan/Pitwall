PitWall v1.0.1 Release Notes 🏁

This update brings important notification enhancements and bug fixes to ensure reliable race session reminders and rich push notifications!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

🔔 Notification Fixes & Enhancements

- Location & Session Name Fix: Resolved a bug where race location names were not showing accurately in local session reminders.
- Rich Media Notifications: Added support for expandable image preview cards (`BigPictureStyle`) for breaking news alerts.
- Deep Linking & Navigation: Tapping on notifications now opens direct in-app destinations (e.g. Standings, Home) or external news articles seamlessly.
- Audio & Channel Priority: Refined custom F1 engine audio alerts and high-priority notification channel setup.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

📲 Installation
1. Download `PitWall-v1.0.1.apk`.
2. Open the file on your Android device.
3. Enjoy the Formula 1 season with accurate race alerts!
