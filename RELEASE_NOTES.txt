PitWall v1.0.0 Release Notes 🏁

We are thrilled to announce the official v1.0.0 release of PitWall! PitWall is a high-performance F1 companion app for Android that combines real-time data, modern glassmorphism aesthetics, push notifications, and offline-first reliability.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

🚀 Key Features & Highlights

🔔 Real-Time Push Notifications & Race Alarms
- Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): Receive instant alerts for breaking news, race control updates, and championship changes.
- Custom F1 Sound Effects: High-octane engine rev notification sounds for session alerts.
- Exact Session Reminders: Set local exact alarms (15m, 30m, 1h prior) for FP, Qualifying, Sprint, and Main Race sessions.

👤 Dynamic Driver & Team Profiles
- 2026 Driver & Team Stats: Points progression charts, podiums, wins, and driver bio details.
- Teammate Head-to-Head: Side-by-side visual comparisons of teammate pairings across qualifying and race results.
- Technical Chassis Specs: Explore team power units, chassis models, and high-res 2026 challenger visuals.

🏁 Detailed Race Results & Standings
- Race Results Breakdown: Full session classification, positions gained/lost, and fastest lap indicators.
- Live Championship Standings: Redesigned Driver and Constructor leaderboards.

📅 Smart System Calendar Sync
- System Calendar Export: Sync Grand Prix weekend session schedules directly to your device's native calendar.
- Flexible Controls: Sync all or future races with one tap, or clear synced events cleanly.

🎨 Premium UI/UX & Customization
- Glassmorphism UI: Ultra-sleek blurred navigation and UI cards powered by the Haze library.
- Dynamic Theming: Switch effortlessly between Light & Dark modes or choose custom accent color schemes.
- F1 News Hub: Aggregated news headlines with expandable reader views.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

🛠️ Technical Architecture & Stack
- Architecture: 100% Kotlin built with Jetpack Compose & Material 3.
- Dependency Injection: Hilt with KSP annotation processing.
- Offline-First: Local caching via Room Database & DataStore Preferences.
- Networking: Retrofit, OkHttp, and Jsoup for web parsing.
- Firebase: Integrated `firebase-messaging` and `firebase-analytics` via Firebase BoM.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

📲 Installation
1. Download `PitWall-v1.0.0.apk`.
2. Open the file on your Android device.
3. Enable "Install from Unknown Sources" if prompted.
4. Launch PitWall and enjoy the Formula 1 season!
