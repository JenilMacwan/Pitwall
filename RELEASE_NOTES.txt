PitWall v1.1.0 Release Notes 🏎️🏁

This major update introduces two brand-new flagship features: Live Team Radio Transmissions and the Apex AI Chatbot Assistant!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

📻 Live Team Radio Transmissions

- Live Audio Clips: Stream real-time team radio transmissions recorded directly from Grand Prix race sessions.
- Driver Filtering: Effortlessly filter audio clips by individual driver chips (e.g. ALL, VER, NOR, LEC, HAM) or view the full session timeline.
- Custom Team-Colored Audio Player: Interactive player cards featuring team-specific accent colors, animated progress bars, and real-time playback duration timers.
- Offline Caching: Cached team radio messages in Room Database for instant loading and offline playback.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

🤖 Apex AI Chatbot Strategy Assistant

- AI-Powered F1 Assistant: Meet Apex — your dedicated AI telemetry and strategy companion for Formula 1 queries.
- Quick Prompt Suggestions: One-tap prompt bar for 2026 Active Aero rules, driver standings, race schedules, and teammate H2H battles.
- Live Telemetry Indicator & Animations: Smooth typing indicators, animated status indicators, and clean session reset options.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

🔔 Performance & Dependency Improvements

- Updated Network Architecture: Refined Retrofit and Hilt network modules for API communication.
- Enhanced Theme & System Bar Support: Polished light/dark status bar and navigation bar transitions across all screens.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

📲 Installation
1. Download `PitWall-v1.1.0.apk`.
2. Open the file on your Android device.
3. Enjoy the Formula 1 season with Live Team Radio and Apex AI!
