PitWall v1.2.0 Release Notes 🏎️🏁

This major update introduces the Next Race Session Timeline, Championship Standings redesign, custom F1 navigation icons, track temperature weather telemetry, Material 3 Snackbar feedback, and first-launch notification permissions.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

🏎️ Home Dashboard & Next Race Card

- Session Schedule Timeline Bar: Added a horizontal weekend schedule timeline bar on the Next Race card showing FP1, FP2, FP3, Qualifying, Sprint, and Grand Prix sessions with live green indicator dots (•), completed session dimming, and F1 Red race highlights.
- Custom F1 Hamburger Menu Icon: Custom 3-bar F1 hamburger navigation icon with an F1 Red middle accent line across all main screens.
- Live Session Status Pill: High-visibility green status pill (`• LIVE: SESSION`) when a Grand Prix session is actively taking place.
- Adaptive Countdown Progress Ring: Progress ring for countdown days, displaying a full 360° red ring on session day (0 Days remaining).
- Track Temperature & Weather Badge: Weather badge now displays both Air Temperature and Track Temperature (`Track 38°C`) with condition-adaptive icon color tinting (Sunny Amber, Rain Blue, Slate Grey).
- Quick Results Navigation: Tapping "View Full Race Result →" on the Podium card navigates directly to the results breakdown of the last completed round.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

📊 Championship Standings 3-Card Redesign

- 3-Card Podium Layout: Redesigned Constructor & Driver top standings into a 3-card layout featuring Championship Leader (P1) and Contenders (P2 & P3).
- Real Data & P1 Telemetry Index: Live standings data with point gap calculations, official constructor team color themes, and dynamic P1 Index progress bars.
- Clean Constructor Driver Names: Constructor driver lineups are formatted to display clean last names (e.g., `Antonelli · Russell`, `Hamilton · Leclerc`).
- Round Progress Pill: Added top bar round progress pill (`R14 / 24`) with a live green indicator dot on the Standings screen.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

📱 Navigation Drawer & System Enhancements

- Grouped Navigation Drawer: Organized side drawer options into structured sections (`RACE HUB`, `UTILITIES`, `PREFERENCES`) with monospaced headers.
- Material 3 Snackbar System: Converted system Toast messages across Schedule, Settings, and Navigation Drawer to Material 3 `Snackbar` overlays for clean in-app feedback.
- First-Launch Notification Permissions: On Android 13+ (API 33+), notification permissions are requested on initial app startup for a seamless setup experience.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

📲 Installation
1. Download `PitWall-v1.2.0.apk`.
2. Open the file on your Android device.
3. Enjoy the Formula 1 season with Live Team Radio, Apex AI, and Real-Time Race Intelligence!
