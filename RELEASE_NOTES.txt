PitWall v1.0.0 Release Notes 🏁

We are thrilled to announce the official v1.0.0 release of PitWall! This version represents a complete overhaul and the "Gold" standard of our F1 companion experience, combining real-time data, premium aesthetics, and professional-grade reliability.

🚀 Key Features

👤 Dynamic Driver & Team Profiles
Dive deep into the grid with our new profile system:
- Live 2026 Stats: Real-time points, podiums, and wins for the current season.
- Teammate Head-to-Head: Visual comparisons of team pairings.
- Technical Specs: Explore chassis details (RB21, SF-25), power units, and team leadership.
- The Challenger: High-quality visuals of the 2026 F1 cars.

🎨 Premium UI/UX & Customization
- Dynamic Theming: Real-time switching between Light and Dark modes.
- Glassmorphism: Modern blur effects on the navigation bar and UI components.
- Refined Standings: Completely redesigned Driver and Constructor leaderboards.
- Smooth Navigation: Persistent controls and optimized scrolling for deep data views.

📅 Smart Calendar Sync
- Device Integration: Sync the entire 2026 schedule directly to your system calendar.
- Intelligent Updates: Options to sync only future races or clear all synced events with one tap.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

🛠️ Under the Hood (Technical)
- Offline-First: Robust local caching using Room (v25) ensures the app works without a connection.
- Quality Assurance: Integrated a comprehensive test suite (Unit & Instrumented) covering ViewModels, Repositories, and DAOs.
- CI/CD Pipeline: Configured GitHub Actions for automated quality checks and artifact generation.
- Architecture: 100% Kotlin with Jetpack Compose, Material 3, and Hilt dependency injection.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

📲 How to Install
1. Download the PitWall-v1.0.0.apk.
2. Open the file on your Android device.
3. If prompted, allow "Installation from Unknown Sources".
4. Open PitWall and enjoy the season!
