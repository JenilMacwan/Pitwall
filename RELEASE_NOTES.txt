PitWall v1.2.1 Release Notes 🏎️🏁

This update introduces the redesigned Race Schedule experience, interactive segmented tab toggles, compact upcoming race cards, dynamic circuit telemetry & track layout mapping, expanded news card styling, and Vico chart analytics upgrades.

🚀 More UI and performance updates coming soon!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

📅 Redesigned Schedule Hub & Race Cards

- Segmented Tab Toggle: Upgraded Schedule filtering tabs into a smooth sliding segmented toggle bar ("All Races", "Upcoming", "Completed") with spring-animated pill indicators.
- Compact Upcoming Race Cards: Introduced a sleek, compact card layout for upcoming Grands Prix featuring round numbers, sprint badges, event date ranges, and interactive "Details ⌄" / "Collapse ↑" expandable session timetables.
- Grand Prix Session Badge: Redesigned the Grand Prix box with an F1 Red squircle trophy icon, red laps pill badge, and date/time formatting converted to the user's local device time zone.
- Circuit Telemetry & Track Layouts: Added a 3-column telemetry section displaying LAPS, CIRCUIT LENGTH, and dynamic track layout diagrams with crisp vertical dividers.
- Circuit Type Badges: Theme-adaptive badges ("STREET CIRCUIT", "PERMANENT CIRCUIT") positioned on race cards.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

📰 News Feed & Visual Enhancements

- Top Bar Surface Alignment: Refactored top bar buttons across News and Schedule screens with symmetrical 38dp circular surfaces for smooth, unclipped refresh progress spinners.
- Expanded News Cards: Enhanced expanded news article styling with subtle outline dividers and call-to-action buttons ("Read Full Article →").

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

📊 Analytics & Performance Upgrades

- Points Progression Chart: Upgraded Vico chart layer interpolation for driver and constructor points progression analytics.
- Offline Circuit Caching: Updated local Room database schema (`CircuitInfoEntity`) to cache circuit length, total laps, circuit type, and SVG layout diagrams for instant offline viewing.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

📲 Installation
1. Download `PitWall-v1.2.1.apk`.
2. Open the file on your Android device.
3. Enjoy the Formula 1 season with Live Team Radio, Apex AI, and Real-Time Race Intelligence!
