PitWall v1.1.2 Release Notes 🏎️🏁

This update brings important bug fixes and stability enhancements to Schedule Calendar Sync, Race & Session Notifications, the Apex AI Strategy Assistant, and the Settings & Privacy suite.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

🗓️ Schedule Calendar Sync Improvements

- Complete Session Sync: Calendar sync now imports all weekend sessions (FP1, FP2, FP3, Sprint Shootout, Sprint Race, Qualifying, and Grand Prix).
- PitWall App Branding: All calendar entries now feature distinct `🏎️ PitWall` event titles and detailed descriptions.
- Accurate Time Parsing & Deduplication: Fixed a glitch where events had random timestamps or failed to appear, and added duplicate event prevention.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

🔔 Notifications & Session Reminders

- Unified Notification Design: All local session alarms and push notifications now feature uniform PitWall branding with the large F1 app icon, signature F1 Red accent, and expanded text layout.
- Audio & Channel Fixes: Standardized notification channel (`f1_alerts_v3`) and custom engine audio alerts (`f1_car_2`) for consistent delivery across all Android versions.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

🤖 Apex AI Chatbot Strategy Assistant

- Connection & Initialization Fixes: Resolved API connection and initialization issues to ensure fast, reliable responses.
- Logging & Diagnostics: Added comprehensive request/response diagnostic logs.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

⚙️ Settings, Privacy & App Information

- Redesigned Privacy Policy: Polished document-style interface featuring key privacy trust guarantees (100% Local-First Data, Zero Data Selling, No Account Needed), numbered policy sections, and direct data protection contact options.
- Open Source Licenses & Data Attribution: Added animated open-source library cards with license badges and clear F1 data source attributions.
- Language & Preferences: UI polish across settings items and enhanced language selection support.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

📲 Installation
1. Download `PitWall-v1.1.2.apk`.
2. Open the file on your Android device.
3. Enjoy the Formula 1 season with Live Team Radio and Apex AI!
