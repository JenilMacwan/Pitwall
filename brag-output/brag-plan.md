---

## 1. Creative Concept & Narrative Arc

* **The Problem:** Formula 1 fans juggle 5 separate disjointed apps every race weekend (streaming for team radio, news sites for articles, calendar apps for session timings, official live timing apps behind $80 paywalls).
* **The Solution:** PitWall — modern, offline-first, beautiful Jetpack Compose glassmorphism UI, 100% free with Live Team Radio, Apex AI strategy assistant, and real-time timing.
* **The Climax:** Closed Beta is officially open on Google Play — First 50 spots available.

---

## 2. Motion Design & 3D Visual System

- **3D Floating Perspective Mockups:** Instead of flat 2D slides, phone mockups are rendered in isometric 3D perspective using matrix projection (`cv2.warpPerspective`), tilting in space with subtle dynamic camera breathing, soft shadows, and edge gloss.
- **Dynamic Particle Speed Lines:** Horizontal racing streaks representing high aerodynamic velocity (`#E11D2A` & `#FFFFFF`).
- **Telemetry Pulse Grid:** Dark high-tech grid background pulsing with live track telemetry signals.
- **Glassmorphic Hud Cards:** Translucent floating cards with blur effects, neon red borders, and glowing badge pills.
- **Gantry Race Start Animation:** 5 countdown lights sequencing with red bloom glows.
